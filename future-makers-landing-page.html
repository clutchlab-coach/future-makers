Questions

Straight answers.

Who is Future Makers for?

Young adults aged 18–29 who want to learn AI-era skills, start an income path, and build in public — whether you’re studying, working a job you want more from, or figuring out your next move. No experience needed; the Starter Guide and the tracks meet you where you are.

Is this a get-rich program?

No. Future Makers is an educational platform and a community. We teach skills, share step-by-step blueprints, and keep you accountable — we make no income guarantees, and anyone who promises you easy money is not being honest with you.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Membership is month to month — you can cancel anytime, and you keep access until the end of the period you’ve paid for. No lock-in contracts.

What happens after I join?

Your welcome pack arrives by email within 24 hours, with your first training session, the blueprint for your chosen track, and an invite to your accountability group and the community.

Do I need to pick a track right away?

No. Start with the free Starter Guide — it walks through all three tracks with first steps for each. Most members pick one track to start and explore the others later.

Honest disclaimer: Future Makers is an educational platform. We provide training, blueprints, and community support — we do not guarantee income, clients, audience growth, or any specific result. What you build depends on your work, your circumstances, and factors outside anyone’s control. Treat every income path here as a skill to learn and a project to work at, not a promise.
